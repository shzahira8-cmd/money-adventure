    Addition of Money & 3D Mini Market

    STEP 1: ADDITION WITHOUT REGROUPING

    Arrange digits vertically according to place value (Thousands, Hundreds, Tens, Ones) and keep the RM symbol aligned.

    RM 2,140.20
    + RM 1,325.45
    RM 3,465.65

    STEP 2: ADDITION WITH REGROUPING (100 SEN = RM1)

    When adding sen exceeds 100 sen, regroup 100 sen into RM1 in the Ones column. Example: RM1,250.70 + RM2,375.60 = RM3,626.30.
